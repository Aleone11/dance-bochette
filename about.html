About the studio

A family studio, three generations strong.

Norma Jeanne (Thaggard) Bochette spent her earliest years dancing barefoot along the dirt roads of 1930s Fort Myers.

As a teen she won state championships in swimming and diving, and became the first girl in Florida history to lead a high school marching band as drum major. At Rollins College she formed a campus dance team — and met L.D. Bochette, Gainesville's football captain.

After graduation the couple moved to New York: L.D. drafted by the New York Giants, Jeanne dancing with the Rockettes at Radio City Music Hall. The Korean War changed their plans.

Seventy-five years, in brief.

Our approach

A lifetime love of the art.

Our training focuses on technical, artistic, and personal development in a supportive, nurturing environment. We place as much emphasis on the development of dancers' characters as we do on the characters they play on stage.

Year-end performances are carefully curated, with well-defined themes and frequent guest artists and choreographers. Supplemental experiences — Florida Dance Masters workshops and selected competitions — are screened to fit our training philosophy.

"We serve generations of families — a testament to our studio's strength."
