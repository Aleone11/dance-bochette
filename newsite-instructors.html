Founder · 1951

Jeanne Bochette

Called "the Dancer's Teacher," her belief in blending dance with each student's goals in life continues in our school.

Her career encompassed New York shows, Ballet Royal, study in the USA and England, and certification in the Cecchetti Ballet System, Tap and Jazz. She received Lifetime Achievement awards from Florida Dance Masters, the Florida Dance Association and Rollins College, and the Alliance "Angel of the Arts" awards for Lifetime Achievement and Teacher of the Year, and was honored as a "Grand Dame" in 2010.
